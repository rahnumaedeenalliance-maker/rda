RAHNUMA E DEEN ALLIANCE

Rahnuma E Deen Alliance (RDA) A Public Charitable Trust dedicated to Social Welfare, Education, and Peace. is a non-discriminatory humanitarian organization committed to uplifting humanity through service, unity, education, and peaceful coexistence. We believe in equality, justice, and positive action for a better and brighter society.

What We Do

Social Welfare

Helping the needy, widows, and orphans in our India with financial and moral support. & Serving the underprivileged with financial, emotional, and practical assistance. Standing beside those in need without discrimination of caste, creed, or background.

Education

Supporting quality education and skill development to build confident, self-reliant individuals and communities.

Dispute Resolution

Promoting harmony, resolving disputes peacefully, and fostering brotherhood through dialogue and mutual respect.

Unity & Social Reform

Encouraging equality, compassion, and positive change by removing discrimination and spreading awareness for a better society.
